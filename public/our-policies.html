Our policies

Welcome to Mein Haus Am See!

‍To ensure all our guests have a comfortable and enjoyable stay, please review our policies carefully.

1. Booking and Reservation Policy
‍
• Check-In Time: 3:00 PM
• Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM
• Payment: All bookings are made through Airbnb and are prepaid. Full payment is required at the time of booking to confirm your reservation.
• Age Requirement: Guests must be 21 years or older to book a reservation.

2. Cancellation Policy
‍
• All cancellations are handled through Airbnb's policy. Please refer to your booking confirmation for details.
• Full Refund: Generally, a full refund is available for cancellations made within 48 hours of booking, if the check-in date is at least 14 days away.
• Partial Refund: A 50% refund is typically offered for cancellations made at least 7 days before check-in. No refunds are provided for cancellations within 7 days of check-in.

3. House Rules
‍
• No Smoking: Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the house. A €200 cleaning fee will be charged for any violation.
• Pets: Pets are allowed for an additional fee of €10 per night. Please notify us at the time of booking if you plan to bring a pet. Owners are responsible for any damages.
• Quiet Hours: Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM to respect our neighbors.
• Maximum Occupancy: The maximum number of guests allowed is 4. Exceeding this limit may result in the cancellation of your stay without a refund.

4. Amenities and Usage

• Wi-Fi: Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.
• Fire Pit & Sauna: Guests are welcome to use the outdoor fire pit and sauna but must adhere to all posted safety guidelines. Use is at your own risk.
• Kitchen: The kitchen is fully equipped. We kindly ask guests to wash all dishes and return items to their original places before check-out.

‍

Falkenhagener See,Brandenburg, Germany
